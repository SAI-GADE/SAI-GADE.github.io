Turbine Blade 3D Modelling & Structural Analysis

3D ModellingAbaqusFEAModal AnalysisFatigue

Overview

During the R&D internship at Triveni Turbines Limited, worked on aerodynamic steam forces and turbine blade power prediction validation. Performed 3D modelling and structural analysis using Abaqus, identified natural frequencies through modal analysis, assessed static/dynamic loading using Campbell and SAFE diagrams, and evaluated fatigue safety margins with Haigh diagrams for HCF prediction.
